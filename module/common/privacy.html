Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are important to us.

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to Farmer First Marketplace. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our marketplace. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, place an order, or use our services, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Delivery address and location information
  • Account login information
  • Products added to your cart
  • Order and transaction details
  • Information provided when contacting customer support
2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to provide and improve our services, including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Processing and managing orders
  • Providing delivery services
  • Connecting buyers with farmers and sellers
  • Sending order confirmations and updates
  • Providing customer support
  • Improving our website and marketplace experience
  • Preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activity
3. Payment Information

Payment information may be processed through authorized payment service providers. We do not intentionally store complete payment card details on our own servers. Payment providers may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to provide our services, such as with:

  • Farmers or sellers involved in fulfilling your order
  • Delivery partners
  • Payment and technology service providers
  • Customer support providers
  • Government or legal authorities when legally required
5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to maintain login sessions, remember preferences, manage shopping carts, and improve the user experience.

7. Your Choices

You may review or update certain account information through your account settings. You may also contact us if you want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable laws and legitimate business requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use online marketplace services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

9. Third-Party Services

Our marketplace may use third-party services for authentication, payments, analytics, hosting, maps, notifications, or delivery. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, or legal requirements. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
